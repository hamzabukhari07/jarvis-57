    HomeStudio

    Nice people. Serious about the work.

    Loudish is a four-person brand and web studio in Bristol. We opened in 2016 to work with the kind of businesses we love to visit: the café with the queue, the hotel with twelve rooms, the label that repairs what it sells.

    Good
    questions.

    What guides the work.

    Listen first

    The best ideas are usually hiding in how owners describe their business on a good day. We spend the first two weeks writing those words down.

    Draw on real things

    We judge a logo on a coffee cup, a key tag and a phone screen, never on a white slide. If it doesn’t work small, it doesn’t work.

    Leave it usable

    A brand you can’t update without us isn’t finished. Every handover includes templates, a guide and an hour of training.

    HomePrivacy

    Privacy notice.

    Short version: we collect very little, we use it only to reply to you and send the newsletter you asked for, and we never sell or share it. Last updated 1 September 2026.

    What we collect How we use it The newsletter Cookies Your rights Contact

    What we collect

    When you send an enquiry we keep your name, email address, business name and the message you wrote. When you join the newsletter we keep your email address and the date you subscribed.

    How we use it

    We use enquiry details to reply to you and, if we work together, to run the project. We keep enquiries for two years and project records for seven years, as UK accounting rules require.

    The newsletter

    We send one email a month. Every email has a one-click unsubscribe link, and unsubscribing deletes your address from the list within 30 days.

    Cookies

    This site uses no advertising or tracking cookies. We count visits with a privacy-friendly analytics tool that stores no personal data.

    Your rights

    Under UK GDPR you can ask to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you, or object to how we use it. We’ll respond within one month.
