      How Halvane got Monday mornings back

      A 14-person brand and packaging studio replaced a whiteboard, two spreadsheets and a weekly planning meeting with Taskmuse. Here is what changed in the first quarter.

      Company
      Halvane
      Industry
      Brand and packaging design
      Team
      14 people, 2 offices
      Plan
      Team, billed yearly
      Rolled out
      March 2026, in two weeks
      Replaced
      Whiteboard, 2 spreadsheets, a weekly meeting

      Halvane designs identities and packaging for food and drink brands. Most weeks the studio runs six to eight client projects at once, with review dates that clients set and rarely move.

      Before: a plan that was out of date by Tuesday

      Every Monday at 9:00 the whole studio met to rebuild the week on a whiteboard. Adaeze Okafor, who leads studio operations, then copied the plan into a spreadsheet and chased updates over chat. "By Tuesday afternoon a client had moved something and the board was fiction," she says.

      Requests arrived everywhere: email threads with clients, the studio chat, the website brief form. Nobody owned the inbox, so small tasks waited until they became urgent.

      Rollout: two weeks, one project at a time

      The studio started with a single client project, Saltmarsh, and connected three calendars and the shared inbox. Muse spent the first week learning how long Halvane's work actually takes: a logo feedback round averages 90 minutes, a dieline revision about two hours.

      Muse has the week drafted before we open our laptops. We spend ten minutes adjusting it, not an hour building it.Adaeze Okafor, Studio operations lead

      What changed

      • Monday planning dropped from 60 minutes to 10. The team reviews Muse's draft and changes two or three placements.
      • Missed client deadlines fell by 31% in the first quarter, because review dates now block time days in advance.
      • Each designer gets about 6.5 hours a week back, mostly from fewer status messages and less context switching.
      • Handoffs are automatic. When a design review is approved, print files go straight to production with the right brief attached.

      What's next

      Halvane is moving client reviews into Taskmuse guest access this autumn, so clients can see review dates without another status email.

        Privacy policy

        Last updated September 14, 2026. The short version: your plans are yours, Muse learns privately and you can export or delete everything.

        On this pageData we collectHow Muse uses dataSharingRetentionYour rightsData processing

        Data we collect

        Account details (name, work email, password hash), workspace content you create or connect (tasks, comments, calendar events, forwarded emails) and product usage events such as which views you open. We do not buy data about you from third parties.

        How Muse uses your data

        Muse ranks, schedules and summarizes using models that run inside your workspace's region. Patterns it learns, such as how long your team takes for a review, stay in your workspace. Your content is never used to train models shared with other customers.

        Sharing

        We share data only with sub-processors that host and operate the service (listed in our data processing addendum), and when the law requires it. We never sell personal data.

        Retention

        Workspace content is kept while your account is active. When you close a workspace, we delete it within 30 days and backups roll off within 90 days.

        Your rights

        You can access, correct, export or delete your data from Settings at any time, or write to privacy@taskmuse.app. We reply within 30 days.

        Data processing addendum

        Team and Business customers can sign our data processing addendum, including standard contractual clauses for transfers outside the EU. Ask our team for a countersigned copy.
