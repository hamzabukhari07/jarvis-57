How a flagship project runs

  2. Weeks 1-2

    Discover

    Stakeholder interviews, an analytics review and a competitor teardown. You get a written brief with goals we can measure.

  3. Weeks 3-5

    Direction

    Two creative routes shown as real pages, not mood boards. We choose one together and test it with your customers.

  4. Weeks 6-12

    Design and build

    Designers and engineers in the same sprint. A demo every Thursday on a staging link, never a slideshow.

  5. Launch and after

    Tune

    We launch, watch the numbers for 30 days and fix what matters. Then we hand over, or stay on as your partner team.
