Home/Privacy

Privacy policy

Effective September 1, 2026. Solvine Labs, Inc.

What we collectHow we use itAI and trainingSharingRetentionYour rights

What we collect

Account details you give us (name, work email, company) and the calendar data you connect. By default we read free or busy times only. Event titles, attendees and locations are read only for calendars where you turn on full access.

How we use it

We use your data to draft and maintain your schedule, to show you and your admins insights about your own workspace, and to run and secure the service. We do not sell personal data and we do not show ads.

AI and model training

Drafting runs on models hosted in our own cloud accounts. Your calendar content is never used to train models that serve other customers. Business customers can turn off all model processing of event titles.

Sharing and subprocessors

We share data only with subprocessors that host, email and support the service, under contracts at least as strict as this policy. The current list is available to every customer on request.

Retention and deletion

  • Disconnecting a calendar removes its cached events within 24 hours.
  • Deleting your account removes your data within 30 days, and from backups within 90.
  • Audit logs on Business are kept for 400 days unless your admin sets a shorter period.

Your rights

You can access, correct, export or delete your data at any time from Settings, or by writing to privacy@solvine.example. We answer within 30 days, usually much sooner.

Accounts

You need a work email to create a workspace. You are responsible for the people you invite and for keeping sign-in links and SSO settings secure.

Trials and billing

Every plan starts with a 14-day trial. Paid plans renew monthly or yearly until cancelled. Seats are billed when active, and seats added mid-cycle are prorated to the day.

Acceptable use

  • Do not use Solvine to send unsolicited invitations or spam.
  • Do not connect calendars you are not authorised to access.
  • Do not try to break, probe or overload the service.

Your content

Your calendars and settings stay yours. You grant us the limited rights needed to run the service for you, and nothing more.

Ending service

You can cancel at any time from Settings. We will keep your data available for export for 30 days after cancellation, then delete it as described in the privacy policy.
