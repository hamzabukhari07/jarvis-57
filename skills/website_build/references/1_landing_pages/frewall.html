    HomeAbout

    A neighbourhood pharmacy on Quayside Row, since 2011

    Fernwell started as one counter and two pharmacists. It is still a pharmacy first: every order, delivery and chat goes through a registered pharmacist in Port Alder.

    Why we deliver

    In our first winter, half the calls we took were from people too unwell to come in. So we started walking prescriptions round the corner ourselves. Today our own couriers cover five neighbourhoods, but the rule has not changed: nothing leaves the counter until a pharmacist has checked it.

    We keep the range short and the advice free. If something cheaper works just as well, we will tell you. If you need a doctor rather than a product, we will tell you that too, and help you get an appointment.

    • 2011Opened on Quayside Row
    • 4Registered pharmacists on the team
    • 5Neighbourhoods with 2-hour delivery
    • 14Hours a day with a pharmacist on chat

    Pharmacist first

    Every order is checked by a pharmacist before it is packed, including vitamins. It takes a few minutes and catches real problems.

    Plain prices

    No dispensing fees, no surprise delivery charges, and the cheaper equivalent when there is one.

    Private by default

    Your health data is used to care for you and nothing else. We never sell it or use it for ads.

      Delivery and returns

      Our own couriers deliver from the counter on Quayside Row, 8am to 8pm, seven days a week. Free over $35 or with the Fernwell Card.

      Where we deliver

      Pick your area in the header and we will show the right times everywhere on the site.

      Sealed, unbranded bags

      Every order goes out in a tamper-evident bag with nothing on the outside that says what is inside. Prescription items need a signature.

      If you are out

      Non-prescription orders can be left in a safe place you choose. Prescription items come back to the pharmacy and we text you to rebook.

      Returns within 30 days

      Unopened, non-medicine items can be returned for a full refund. For safety, medicines cannot be resold, so we only take them back if they were faulty or sent in error.

      Privacy notice

      Last updated 1 September 2026

      What we collectHow we use itWho can see itYour rights

      Fernwell Pharmacy (“we”) runs the pharmacy at 41 Quayside Row, Port Alder, this website and the Fernwell app. This notice explains what we do with your information. It is written for a fictional pharmacy and is here as an example layout.

      What we collect

      Your name, contact details and delivery address; your order history; prescriptions you send us or that your surgery sends on your behalf; notes from consultations; and, if you choose, your date of birth and allergies so we can check medicines safely.

      How we use it

      To dispense and deliver your medicines, to give you advice that fits your health, to send reminders you ask for and to meet our legal duties as a registered pharmacy. We do not use your health information for advertising and we never sell it.

      Who can see it

      Only the pharmacy team caring for you, our couriers (name, address and phone only) and the services that host our systems under contract. Your doctor may receive information when you ask us to share it or when your safety requires it.

      Your rights

      You can ask for a copy of your information, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete anything we are not required to keep. Write to care@fernwell.example or ask at the counter.

      HomeTerms

      Terms of sale

      Last updated 1 September 2026

      OrdersPharmacist checksPrices and paymentReturns

      These terms apply to orders placed with Fernwell Pharmacy online, in the app or by phone. They are an example for a fictional pharmacy.

      Orders

      Your order is a request to buy. A contract is formed when a pharmacist approves the order and it leaves the store. We will tell you straight away if anything cannot be supplied.

      Pharmacist checks

      Our pharmacists may ask questions before supplying some medicines, limit quantities, or decline a sale if it is not safe for you. When that happens you are not charged for the item.

      Prices and payment

      Prices include sales tax where it applies. Your card is authorised when you order and charged when the order is dispatched. Delivery is $3.90 for orders under $35 and free above that or with the Fernwell Card.

      Returns

      Unopened, non-medicine items can be returned within 30 days. Medicines can only be returned if they were faulty or sent in error. See delivery and returns for the full policy.
